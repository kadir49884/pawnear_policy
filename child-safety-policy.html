Child Safety Standards Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2025

⚠️ Zero Tolerance Policy: PawNear enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any violation will result in immediate account termination and reporting to relevant authorities.

1. Our Commitment

PawNear is a platform dedicated to helping street animals. We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and strictly prohibit any content or behavior related to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child exploitation.

2. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on PawNear:

  • Any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Content that sexualizes, grooms, or exploits children
  • Inappropriate interaction with minors
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing content that endangers children
  • Using the platform to contact or communicate with minors for inappropriate purposes

3. Age Restrictions

PawNear is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.

4. Content Moderation

We implement the following measures to ensure child safety:

  • User Reports: All users can report inappropriate content or behavior
  • Manual Review: Reported content is reviewed by our moderation team
  • Immediate Action: Violations result in immediate content removal and account suspension
  • Automated Detection: We monitor uploaded images and user interactions

5. Reporting Mechanism

If you encounter any content or behavior that violates child safety standards, please report it immediately:

  • In-App Report: Use the report button on any post or comment
  • Email: kadir49884@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: "Child Safety Report - Urgent"

All reports are treated with the highest priority and confidentiality.

6. Our Response Protocol

When we receive a report regarding child safety:

  • Immediate Review: Reports are reviewed within 24 hours
  • Content Removal: Violating content is removed immediately
  • Account Termination: Offending accounts are permanently banned
  • Law Enforcement: We cooperate fully with law enforcement and report CSAM to:
    • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
    • Local authorities as required by law
    • Relevant international child protection organizations

7. User Responsibilities

All PawNear users must:

  • Use the platform only for its intended purpose (helping street animals)
  • Report any suspicious or inappropriate content immediately
  • Not share personal information with unknown users
  • Follow community guidelines and terms of service

8. Prevention Measures

PawNear implements these preventive measures:

  • User authentication through Google Sign-In
  • Content monitoring and review system
  • Ability to report and block users
  • No private messaging between users
  • Public comments only (no direct private communication)
  • Geographic location is approximate (city/district level only)

9. Compliance

PawNear complies with:

  • Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • Local laws regarding child protection

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in legal requirements or our practices. Users will be notified of significant changes through the app.
